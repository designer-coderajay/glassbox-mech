# Impressum (legal notice): template, NOT yet published

Status: **fill in before publishing.** This file lives outside `docs/`, so it is not served by
the website. Once the fields below are filled, it becomes `docs/impressum.html` and is linked
from the footer and from `docs/privacy.html` (which already points to `impressum.html`).

Why it is needed: the website offers paid plans (pricing section with Pro and Enterprise), so
it is a business-like online service and falls under the provider-identification duty in
§ 5 Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz (DDG, which replaced § 5 TMG in May 2024). A private hobby page
would not need one; a page advertising paid plans does. This is not legal advice; if unsure,
check with a lawyer or your chamber of commerce.

Required fields (§ 5 DDG):

```
Angaben gemäß § 5 DDG

Ajay Pravin Mahale
<Straße und Hausnummer>          <- must be a real postal address where you can be served
<PLZ Ort>                           documents; a P.O. box is not enough
Deutschland

Kontakt
E-Mail: mahale.ajay01@gmail.com
Telefon: <optional, but a second fast contact channel is required; a phone number or a
          contact form that is answered quickly both work>

Umsatzsteuer-ID: <only if you have one; omit otherwise>

Verantwortlich für den Inhalt nach § 18 Abs. 2 MStV:
Ajay Pravin Mahale, <same address>
```

Notes:

- If you register a business (Gewerbe) or a company later, the Impressum must name the
  business and its legal form.
- If you don't want your home address public, the usual option is a paid "ladungsfähige
  Anschrift" (business address service). A P.O. box or email alone is not sufficient.
- Before publishing, also re-check your visa conditions for self-employed activity if the
  paid plans ever start charging money.
