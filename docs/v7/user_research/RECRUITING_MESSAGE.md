# Recruiting message (for the five interviews)

Send it personally, to people you can reach through your own network or communities you
belong to. Do not mass-send it, and do not use scraped contact lists. Edit the
bracketed parts before sending.

---

**Short version (DM / chat)**

> Hi [name], I'm researching how engineers debug LLM, RAG or agent systems when they
> suddenly behave differently, e.g. after a model, prompt or framework change. Have you
> had to track one of those down in the last few months? If so, could I ask you about it
> for 25 minutes? I'm not selling anything: it's research for my open-source
> interpretability work. Notes are anonymised and nothing is recorded unless you agree.
> Totally fine to say no.

**Longer version (email or community post)**

> Subject: 25 min on debugging an AI behaviour change? (research, not a sales call)
>
> Hi [name / all],
>
> I'm Ajay, [an MSc AI graduate / finishing my MSc in AI], working on open-source tools for understanding why AI
> systems behave the way they do. I'm trying to learn how engineers actually
> investigate it when an LLM, RAG or agent system starts behaving differently.
>
> I'm looking for 5 people who have debugged a case like that in the last ~6 months
> and would talk me through what happened, in about 25 minutes, online.
>
> - It's about your real experience; there's nothing to prepare.
> - I won't pitch anything.
> - Notes are anonymised (no names or companies), nothing is recorded unless you agree,
>   and you can ask me to delete your notes anytime.
> - Happy to share the anonymised summary of what I learn afterwards.
>
> If you're up for it, reply with a couple of times that suit you. Thanks!
> Ajay

---

**Notes.**
- **Incentive:** offering the anonymised summary is a fair, free incentive. Do not
  promise anything you cannot deliver.
- **Recording:** if someone agrees to recording, store it outside the repo and delete it
  once the notes are written.
- **Who's in:** track who was asked and who replied only in a private file outside the
  repo. In the repo, participants exist only as P1…P5.
